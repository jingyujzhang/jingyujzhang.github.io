Welcome!

I am on the 2026–27 academic job market, with an expected graduation date of May 2027.

My research examines how accounting standards, technology, and investors affect the people inside and around firms, and how those people, in turn, reveal information about the firms. I work along two lines: information and labor markets, which asks who firms hire, promote, and keep; and entrepreneurship and private capital, which asks how reporting rules written for public firms reach young and private ones. I use archival methods, textual analysis, and machine learning.

My job market paper, Patient Capital, Impatient Reporting: How Accounting Standards Reach the Startup Economy, asks whether a standard aimed at public-firm reporting reaches the private startups that corporate venture capital investors back. It received the Best Paper Award at the 2026 Rutgers Accounting Doctoral Symposium.
